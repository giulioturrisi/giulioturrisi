I am a postdoctoral researcher at the [Dynamic Legged Systems](https://dls.iit.it/) at the Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia. My research focuses on machine learning and optimal control for robot locomotion and manipulation, particularly for quadruped robots.

I received my Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering from the University of Pisa in 2016, and my Master’s degree in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics from Sapienza University of Rome in 2018. In 2022, I completed my PhD in Automatic Control, Bioengineering, and Operations Research at Sapienza, where I was part of the [DIAG Robotics Lab](https://www.diag.uniroma1.it/~labrob/).

I like open-source software, reproducible research, and teaching. Whether you’re interested in a master’s thesis, an internship, or a research collaboration, feel free to get in touch!
